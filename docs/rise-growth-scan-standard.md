Scope: all future RISE growth-scan web reports. Approved reference: https://resources.risedtc.com/scan/yananails-f9/ as reviewed 14 October 2026. This is a reusable visual and delivery standard, not permission to reuse Yananails findings for other businesses. Sections 1-6 remain the rules for post images. For scan web composition specifically, large gold #ffc71d section planes, warm neutral backgrounds and a dark supporting section are permitted, matching the approved report. Use Sora display headings, Manrope body type, near-black text and generous spacing; preserve real screenshots and genuine product/author photographs.

Flow: a large plain-language title and short introduction stating what was reviewed; the strongest one or two useful source-backed findings; RISE introduction and sourced client proof; Mattan's genuine photo and a 30-minute booking CTA; secondary ideas in a closed disclosure. Every insight states the observed fact, why the proposed change is relevant, and one concrete change. The report starts with context before advice. Headlines should be short and useful, body copy prominent and readable, with bold emphasis and layouts chosen for the finding. Avoid repeated text cards, long technical subtext and a generic "What we will test first" opening. Sources, original quotes, metric definitions and experiment details remain accessible in expandable sections. Explain specialist terms in everyday words.

Research: assess the actual offer, buying model, product pages, public ads and recorded ad-to-page destinations. Prioritize substantial paid acquisition and buying-decision opportunities. Include a visible public-ad section with source links and observation dates when available; distinguish Meta active counts from Google archived creative records. Missing or blocked ad data is unknown, never zero. Counts alone cannot establish spend, fatigue, winners, sales or profit. Destination claims require the actual recorded ad link, preserving variants and query strings. A proposed destination or test must be labelled as untested. Never invent defects, private performance, uplift, customer behaviour or pricing. No organic posting, SEO, email or SMS advice. Secondary handling or shipping tweaks cannot carry an otherwise weak scan. One substantial finding is acceptable; no fixed product-pair, shipping, defect or ad quota. Every factual premise needs its relevant original source. Screenshot/product assets must belong to this business and the exact source discussed; retain dates and fallback source links if an image fails.

Conversion: explain RISE's Meta/Google campaigns, performance creative, purchase-page work and financial-health approach. Published client results are separate engagements with their actual case links, never expected results for the prospect. The call reviews actual ad costs, product margins, the proposed paid test and whether RISE's performance model fits. CTA: "Get 30 min with Mattan". Use the current HubSpot booking link and genuine Mattan photograph from the report template. Both current commercial models have fixed fees; never claim $0, no retainer, pure performance or only paid on growth. Genuine video may be reused only after checking current claims and terms. The approved 76-second RISE method excerpt has native controls and captions, stays behind an optional disclosure, and never autoplays; its older full video contains superseded terms and must not replace the excerpt.

Experience: varied source, product, ad and action layouts; large readable headings and body text; useful comparison controls only when both source assets exist. Native mobile scrolling, scoped desktop smoothing, restrained reveal/parallax motion and working section links. Respect reduced motion; all content stays visible without animation/JavaScript and in static prerenders. No sticky-scroll trap, blank screenshot blocks, clipped headlines or horizontal overflow. Preserve accessible focus, keyboard disclosures, reduced-motion behavior and readable loading/error states.

Release: generation emits subject-bound `rise-standard-2026-10-14` presentation using the exact reviewed test copy. The independent whole-report review checks factual support, business usefulness, actionability, coherence, citations and readability. Approval follows final review and image recovery, including repair/loss checks. Unavailable review or broken evidence holds delivery. Render at 375px and 1440px, test missing ads/one insight/extra ideas/rejected review, check static visibility, photo/video/captions and booking links, then verify the live renderer and builder. Legacy reports remain supported. A polished page alone is not a useful scan.
